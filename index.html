Recruitment & Selection Policy

TalentBridge follows a structured, unbiased, and merit-based hiring framework aligned with best HR practices.

📋 Policy Overview & Objectives

TalentBridge's Recruitment & Selection Policy ensures a transparent, equitable, and efficient process for attracting and retaining top talent. Our approach is driven by the following core objectives:

  • Attract qualified candidates aligned with organizational culture and values
  • Ensure fair, bias-free evaluation based on merit and competency
  • Maintain compliance with equal opportunity employment laws (EEO, PWD Act)
  • Optimize time-to-hire and cost-per-hire metrics
  • Build a diverse and inclusive workforce pipeline
  • Ensure a positive candidate experience throughout the hiring journey

🔄 Recruitment Process – Step by Step

1
Manpower Requisition

Department head raises a Manpower Requisition Form (MRF), reviewed and approved by HR Head and Finance. Approval within 3 working days.

2
Job Analysis & JD Preparation

HR team conducts job analysis, prepares Job Description (JD) and Job Specification (JS) in collaboration with the hiring manager. Defines KPIs and competency framework.

3
Sourcing & Advertising

Positions are posted on TalentBridge Portal, LinkedIn, Naukri, and via employee referrals. Campus recruitment conducted for entry-level roles. Target: Sourcing within 5 days.

4
Application Screening & Shortlisting

HR screens resumes against minimum qualifications. ATS (Applicant Tracking System) used for volume filtering. Shortlist shared with hiring manager within 7 days of posting.

5
Assessment & Testing

Role-specific assessments: technical tests for engineering roles, case studies for consulting/finance, psychometric tests for leadership roles. All conducted objectively.

6
Panel Interviews

Structured behavioural and competency-based interviews (STAR method). Minimum 2 interviewers. Scoring via standardized rubric. HR interview for culture fit. Target: 3–5 working days.

7
Background Verification

Employment history, educational credentials, reference checks, and where applicable, criminal record checks through third-party agencies. 5–7 working days.

8
Offer & Onboarding

Offer letter issued with CTC breakup, joining date, and role details. Structured 30-60-90 day onboarding plan provided. HR SPOC assigned for new joiner.

⚖️ Equal Opportunity & Compliance

Non-Discrimination Policy: TalentBridge does not discriminate on basis of gender, religion, caste, nationality, disability, or age. All hiring decisions are purely merit-based.

Diversity Targets: Minimum 33% women candidates in shortlists, active PWD inclusion, and LGBTQ+ affirming hiring practices.

Compliance Framework: Aligned with Factories Act, Contract Labour Act, and Equal Remuneration Act. POSH awareness embedded in onboarding.

Data Privacy: Candidate data handled under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (2023). Data retained for 1 year only.

Compensation Policy & Salary Structures

Market-competitive, performance-linked compensation packages with transparent breakdowns for each role.

🎯 Compensation Philosophy

Pay-for-Performance: Variable components tied to KPIs, team performance, and company profitability. Annual appraisal cycles with merit increments of 8–18%.

Market Benchmarking: Compensation benchmarked against Mercer & Aon salary surveys annually. Target: P50–P75 of market median.

Total Rewards: CTC includes fixed pay, variable pay, benefits, and long-term incentives (ESOPs for leadership roles).

Pay Equity: Regular internal equity audits conducted. Equal pay for equal work regardless of gender or background.
